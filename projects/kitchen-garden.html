Kitchen Garden

An automated vertical hydroponic farming system designed for modern homes, integrating lighting, nutrient management, water circulation and smart automation for efficient soil-free cultivation.

IoT Automation Hydroponics Product R&D
Overview

Automated hydroponic cultivation for modern homes.

Kitchen Garden is a product-development project focused on creating a compact vertical hydroponic farming system for residential environments.

The system is designed to support soil-free cultivation while integrating essential functions such as lighting, nutrient management, water circulation and automation into a single platform.

The project explores how engineering, automation and controlled-environment agriculture can be combined to make small-scale food cultivation more accessible within modern living spaces.

Project Motivation

Making home cultivation more controlled and accessible.

Traditional home gardening depends on suitable outdoor space, soil conditions and regular manual maintenance.

A vertical hydroponic system provides an alternative approach by growing plants without soil and by using a controlled water and nutrient environment.

Kitchen Garden investigates how these cultivation requirements can be integrated with automation to create a compact system intended for modern homes.

System Concept

Integrating cultivation, circulation and automation.

The Kitchen Garden concept combines multiple subsystems required for hydroponic cultivation within a vertical growing platform.

Technical Approach

A multidisciplinary product-development system.

Hydroponic Cultivation

The system uses a soil-free growing approach in which water and nutrient delivery form the foundation of plant cultivation.

Water Circulation

Water circulation is integrated into the system to support continuous delivery of water and nutrients throughout the vertical growing structure.

Controlled Lighting

Artificial lighting is incorporated into the growing environment to support plant cultivation where natural lighting may not be sufficient.

Smart Automation

Automation is explored to reduce the amount of repetitive manual intervention required for operating the growing system.

Product Development

Designing a complete home-growing system.

Kitchen Garden is approached as a complete product rather than as an isolated hydroponics experiment.

The project brings together the physical growing structure, water-management system, lighting, automation and user-oriented product design into an integrated platform.
