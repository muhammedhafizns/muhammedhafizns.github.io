Intelligent Public Safety Drone

An edge-AI-enabled drone research project exploring Vision-Language Models, computer vision and onboard intelligent processing using NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano.

Edge AI Vision-Language Models NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano Computer Vision
Overview

Intelligent aerial systems powered by edge AI.

The Intelligent Public Safety Drone is a research and development project focused on integrating artificial intelligence with an unmanned aerial platform.

The project explores how computer vision and Vision-Language Models can be used to interpret visual information captured by a drone and provide meaningful understanding of the surrounding environment.

NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano is used as the edge computing platform for exploring onboard AI processing and intelligent decision-support capabilities.

Project Motivation

Moving beyond conventional aerial monitoring.

Conventional drones provide powerful aerial observation capabilities. However, interpreting the visual information captured by these systems often depends heavily on human operators or external processing systems.

This project investigates how artificial intelligence can be integrated directly into the aerial platform to support local visual understanding and intelligent interpretation.

The broader research direction is to explore how aerial sensing, computer vision and modern multimodal AI models can work together within an intelligent drone system.

Technical Approach

Combining visual perception with edge intelligence.

Edge AI

AI processing is explored directly on the onboard computing platform, enabling visual information to be processed close to where it is captured.

Computer Vision

Computer-vision techniques provide the foundation for analyzing imagery captured by the drone and extracting useful information from the surrounding environment.

Vision-Language Models

Vision-Language Models are explored as a way to combine visual information with language-based interpretation and higher-level scene understanding.

Intelligent Decision Support

The project explores how information generated from visual AI systems can support meaningful interpretation and decision-making.

System Concept

From aerial imagery to intelligent information.

At a conceptual level, visual information captured by the drone is processed by the onboard edge-AI platform before being interpreted by computer-vision and Vision-Language Model components.
