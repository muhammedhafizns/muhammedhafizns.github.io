Overview

A unified hub for connected smart-home devices.

Deyoze is an IoT home-automation project focused on creating a centralized platform for controlling and managing smart-home devices.

The project explores interoperability between connected devices using the Matter protocol, with the aim of bringing multiple smart-home systems together within a unified environment.

The broader goal is to simplify interaction between different connected devices and provide a more integrated home-automation experience.

Project Motivation

Connecting fragmented smart-home ecosystems.

Smart-home environments can include devices from different manufacturers and platforms, each with their own methods of communication and control.

Deyoze explores how a centralized hub can help bring these devices together and provide a more consistent way to manage connected systems.

The Matter protocol forms a key part of this concept by supporting interoperability between compatible smart-home devices.

Core Capabilities

Centralized control and device interoperability.

Centralized Control

The system is designed around a central hub that can bring multiple smart-home devices together within a unified control environment.

Matter Protocol

Matter is explored as the communication standard for improving interoperability between compatible smart-home devices.

Device Interoperability

The project focuses on enabling devices from different systems to work together more effectively within a connected-home environment.

Systems Engineering

Deyoze brings together communication, device integration and centralized control as parts of a complete home-automation system.

System Concept

Bringing connected devices into one platform.

At a conceptual level, Deyoze acts as a central point between multiple smart-home devices and the user-facing control system.

IoT Architecture

Building around connected systems.

The Deyoze concept is based on the idea of connecting multiple devices through a common automation hub.

Rather than treating each device independently, the project explores how connected systems can be coordinated through a shared control layer.

Interoperability

A shared environment for different devices.

A central focus of Deyoze is interoperability: the ability of different smart-home devices and systems to operate together within the same environment.

By exploring the Matter protocol, the project investigates how device integration can be simplified across a connected-home ecosystem.
