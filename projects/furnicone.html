Overview

AI-assisted furniture product creation.

Furnicone is an AI-powered product platform designed to simplify the process of preparing furniture products for digital listing and visualization.

The platform explores how artificial intelligence and computer vision can support multiple stages of the furniture-listing workflow, including image generation, visual enhancement, dimension estimation and product-description generation.

The broader goal is to bring several AI-assisted product-listing capabilities together within a unified platform.

Project Motivation

Simplifying digital furniture presentation.

Preparing furniture products for online presentation can involve several separate tasks, from producing suitable product imagery to preparing dimensions and written descriptions.

Furnicone explores how artificial intelligence can assist with these tasks and reduce the amount of repetitive work involved in preparing a furniture product for digital presentation.

Core Capabilities

Multiple AI functions within one workflow.

Multi-View Image Generation

The platform explores AI-assisted generation of multiple visual views of a furniture product for improved digital presentation.

Dimension Estimation

Computer-vision-based approaches are explored for estimating useful dimensional information from furniture imagery.

Texture Enhancement

Image-processing and AI techniques are explored to improve the visual appearance and representation of furniture textures.

Automated Descriptions

AI-generated product descriptions are explored as part of the workflow for preparing furniture products for listing.

System Concept

From product imagery to digital listing.

Furnicone brings several AI-assisted operations together around a furniture product and its visual information.
