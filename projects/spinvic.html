Overview

AI-assisted sports performance analysis.

Spinvic is a sports-technology project exploring the use of artificial intelligence and computer vision for analyzing athlete movement from video.

The project focuses on extracting and interpreting information related to posture, body angles, movement patterns and player performance.

The broader objective is to explore how video-based analysis can support more personalized performance evaluation and feedback.

Project Motivation

Turning sports video into useful performance information.

Sports training and performance evaluation often involve detailed observation of how an athlete moves and performs.

Spinvic explores how artificial intelligence can assist this process by analyzing video and extracting information related to movement, posture and body geometry.

The aim is to investigate a technology-driven approach to performance analysis that can support personalized feedback.

Core Capabilities

Understanding athletic movement from video.

Posture Analysis

The platform explores analysis of athlete posture from video to support a clearer understanding of body position during movement.

Body-Angle Analysis

Body-angle information is explored as part of the video-analysis process for understanding player movement and form.

Movement Patterns

The project investigates how patterns of movement can be identified and interpreted from recorded sports video.

Performance Analysis

Movement-related information can be brought together to support analysis of player performance and personalized feedback.

System Concept

From sports video to performance feedback.

At a conceptual level, Spinvic begins with recorded player video and uses AI-based visual analysis to extract information about movement and body positioning.

Computer Vision

Extracting movement information from video.

Computer vision forms the visual-analysis foundation of the Spinvic concept.

The project explores how information contained in sports video can be processed to study player posture, body angles and movement patterns.

Performance Feedback

Supporting individualized analysis.

One direction of the project is to use movement-related information extracted from video as the basis for personalized player performance analysis.

The goal is to explore how AI-assisted visual analysis can contribute to more structured performance feedback.
